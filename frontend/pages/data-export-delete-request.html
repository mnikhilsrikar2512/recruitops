Data Export/Delete Requests

Get a copy of everything your company holds in RecruitOps, or ask for all of it to be deleted. A single candidate's request (export or erase) is handled from that candidate's page.

Export all company data

A zip with every record (one file per table, AI run logs included) and the stored documents. It takes a few minutes; the download works for 7 days.

Delete all company data

Permanent. Another admin must approve it (if you are the only admin, you confirm with your two-step verification code). It then runs after 7 days, and can be cancelled until then. Audit records are kept for 90 days after the deletion.
