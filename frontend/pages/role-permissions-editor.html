info Admin Role

The Admin role has full system access including all permissions across Candidates, Requisitions, Interviews, Offers, Reports, and Settings. Admins can manage users, configure system settings, and perform all read, write, delete, and administrative operations. This role is intended for system administrators and team leads who need unrestricted access to the recruitment platform.
