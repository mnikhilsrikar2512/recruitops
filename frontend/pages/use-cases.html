Questions

Common questions.

Can each client have different hiring stages?

Yes. Your company has a default stage list, and any client can have its own. When a client's list changes, RecruitOps asks where candidates in removed stages should go, so nothing is lost.

We don't pay signing bonuses. Can we turn that off?

Yes. Signing bonuses can be switched on or off for your company, and separately for each client you hire for.

What happens when a client puts a role on hold?

The role is frozen with all its candidates and history intact. Its clock pauses too, so time-to-fill reports stay fair. Resume it whenever the client is ready.

Is our data kept separate from other companies?

Yes. Every company's records are kept apart, and admins sign in with two-step verification.

Can we get our data out?

Yes. Admins can export the company's data, and a single candidate's data can be exported or erased on request.

How do we get started?

Create your company account, confirm your email, and follow the setup checklist on your home screen: offices, team, stages, clients and your first requisition.
