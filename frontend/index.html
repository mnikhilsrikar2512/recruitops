Trust and control

Built to be trusted with candidate data.

Recruitment data is personal data. RecruitOps treats it that way, from the first screen to the last export.

  • Two-step sign-in — admins confirm every sign-in with a code from their phone.
  • Your data stays yours — each company's records are kept apart from every other company's.
  • Full audit trail — sign-ins, changes and decisions are logged and searchable.
  • Export or delete — export your company's data, or a single candidate's, when asked.
  • Safe file uploads — resumes and documents are checked for malware before anyone opens them.
  • Sessions you control — see where you're signed in and sign out other devices.

Questions

What people usually ask first.

Is our company's data kept separate from other companies on RecruitOps?

Yes. RecruitOps is built multi-tenant from the ground up — every company's requisitions, candidates and records are kept apart from every other company's, with no shared visibility between them.

Can agencies use a different hiring process for each client?

Yes. Alongside your company's own default stage list, you can set up a separate stage list per client — add a client interview round, shorten the process, or map stages differently for reporting — without disturbing anyone else's setup.

Does RecruitOps support two-step sign-in?

Yes. Admin accounts can enroll in two-step verification, confirming sign-in with a one-time code in addition to their password.

What happens to candidates if a client puts a role on hold?

Putting a role on hold pauses it without losing anything — candidates already in the pipeline, their stage history and all related activity stay exactly where they were, ready to pick back up.

Can we export or delete our data?

Yes. You can export your company's data, or a single candidate's record, and request deletion when needed.
