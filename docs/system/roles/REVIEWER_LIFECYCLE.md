# Reviewer Lifecycle

## Purpose

The `reviewer` role participates in a limited, contextual part of the workflow without owning the entire recruiting process.

## Typical Customer Title Mappings

- Hiring Manager
- Interviewer
- Functional Reviewer
- Compliance Reviewer
- Department Head

## Main Responsibilities

- review assigned requisitions or candidates
- provide structured feedback
- approve or reject within limited scope
- support decisions at specific workflow checkpoints

## Primary Records Owned Or Controlled

- assigned review tasks
- interview feedback
- approval decisions
- review comments

## Daily Lifecycle

### Step 1. Review assigned items

- open assigned requisition or candidate reviews
- inspect the context shared into the workflow
- understand decision deadline and expected action

### Step 2. Provide feedback or decision

- submit feedback on candidate quality or job fit
- approve, reject, or request clarification when permitted
- provide structured remarks

### Step 3. Complete assigned review

- mark review as completed
- hand decision back to delivery workflow

## Weekly Lifecycle

- review pending feedback count
- complete overdue reviews
- review recurring decision bottlenecks

## Monthly Lifecycle

- review average feedback timeliness
- review participation level where relevant

## Key Screens And Views

- assigned review inbox
- candidate review detail
- requisition review detail
- feedback form or decision form

## Important Handoffs

- receives context-specific work from `delivery_lead`, `client_manager`, or internal intake workflow
- returns feedback into the delivery workflow
- does not own the broader pipeline unless separately assigned another role

## Success Metrics

- review completion time
- low overdue feedback count
- quality and usefulness of structured feedback

## MVP Expectations

For MVP, this role should be able to:

- see assigned review context only
- provide structured feedback
- approve or reject within limited scope when applicable
