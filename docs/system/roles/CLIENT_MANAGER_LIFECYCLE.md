# Client Manager Lifecycle

## Purpose

The `client_manager` role owns active client relationships, requirement intake, follow-up discipline, and account-level execution on the client side.

## Typical Customer Title Mappings

- Account Manager
- Client Partner
- Account Lead
- Business Development Manager
- Client Success Lead

## Main Responsibilities

- manage clients and contacts
- run client meetings and follow-ups
- convert client demand into requisitions
- maintain account notes and expectations
- track client-side SLA and response delays

## Primary Records Owned Or Controlled

- client companies
- client contacts
- meetings
- follow-up actions
- client-driven requisitions
- account notes

## Daily Lifecycle

### Step 1. Review account dashboard

- review meetings due today
- review follow-ups overdue
- review requisitions pending client clarification
- review account warnings and stalled clients

### Step 2. Run client communication

- contact clients or prospects
- conduct meetings or follow-up calls
- confirm hiring demand, deadlines, and expectations

### Step 3. Record business activity

- log meeting details
- capture attendance and outcome
- assign follow-up owner
- set follow-up due date

### Step 4. Convert demand into requisition

- create external requisition when client confirms need
- link direct client and end-client where relevant
- capture SLA, urgency, and hiring context
- assign or notify delivery owner

### Step 5. Track account-side blockers

- note delayed client feedback
- note changed expectations
- note duplicate-profile complaints
- note requisition closures or pauses from client side

## Weekly Lifecycle

- review all active accounts
- review client responsiveness
- review new requisitions created
- review open commercial risks or stalled mandates

## Monthly Lifecycle

- review account performance
- review mandate conversion and retention
- review weak-response clients
- review location or team distribution of client load

## Key Screens And Views

- client list and client profile
- client contacts
- meetings and follow-up views
- account dashboard
- requisition intake from client flow

## Important Handoffs

- hands requisitions to `delivery_lead`
- collaborates with `business_lead` on strategic accounts
- receives delivery updates from `delivery_lead`

## Success Metrics

- follow-up completion rate
- meeting-to-requisition conversion
- client response turnaround
- active-mandate continuity
- low client-escalation backlog

## MVP Expectations

For MVP, this role should be able to:

- manage clients and contacts
- log meetings and follow-ups
- create external requisitions
- view owned account performance
