# Delivery Lead Lifecycle

## Purpose

The `delivery_lead` role owns recruiting execution quality, team allocation, and the operational performance of assigned requisitions.

## Typical Customer Title Mappings

- Lead Recruiter
- Delivery Manager
- Team Lead
- Recruitment Lead
- Talent Delivery Lead

## Main Responsibilities

- own delivery-side requisitions
- allocate work to recruiters and sourcing support
- review pipeline health
- manage blockers and escalations
- maintain delivery quality before client submission

## Primary Records Owned Or Controlled

- requisitions
- recruiter assignments
- sourcing support assignments
- candidate submission readiness
- delivery dashboards
- blocker and SLA-risk views

## Daily Lifecycle

### Step 1. Review delivery dashboard

- review open requisitions
- review aging roles
- review overdue candidate movement
- review recruiter and sourcer load

### Step 2. Allocate and rebalance work

- assign requisitions to recruiters
- assign sourcing support where needed
- shift work when one user is overloaded
- set priority on critical requisitions

### Step 3. Review candidate quality

- inspect shortlisted candidates
- review fit, completeness, and readiness
- decide whether to proceed, refine, or reject

### Step 4. Manage blockers

- identify missing client feedback
- identify low sourcing output
- identify poor stage movement
- escalate issues to `client_manager` or `business_lead` where required

### Step 5. Monitor progress to closure

- review interviews, offers, joins, and stalled roles
- monitor close-rate and SLA risk

## Weekly Lifecycle

- review team productivity
- review requisition aging
- review submission-to-interview ratio
- review blocked roles and action plans
- review recruiter and sourcer workload balance

## Monthly Lifecycle

- review fulfillment rates
- review team conversion ratios
- review low-performing workflows
- review team-level coaching needs

## Key Screens And Views

- delivery dashboard
- requisition list and detail
- team workload view
- candidate readiness views
- stage movement reports

## Important Handoffs

- receives requisitions from `client_manager` or internal intake workflow
- assigns work to `recruiter` and `sourcing_support`
- provides delivery updates back to `client_manager`
- escalates strategic issues to `business_lead`

## Success Metrics

- requisition fill-rate
- aging reduction
- SLA adherence
- recruiter productivity
- submission-to-interview conversion

## MVP Expectations

For MVP, this role must be able to:

- own requisitions
- assign recruiters
- review candidates
- monitor team work queues
- track delivery progress
