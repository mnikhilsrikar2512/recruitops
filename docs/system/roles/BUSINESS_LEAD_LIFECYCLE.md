# Business Lead Lifecycle

## Purpose

The `business_lead` role owns business growth, top-level account performance, and commercial visibility across client-facing operations.

This role focuses on business outcomes rather than day-to-day recruiting execution.

## Typical Customer Title Mappings

- Director
- VP Sales
- Head of Business Development
- BDM Head
- Business Head

## Main Responsibilities

- review prospect and client pipeline
- monitor business development outcomes
- review mandate conversion
- oversee major account health
- intervene in strategic or commercial issues

## Primary Records Owned Or Controlled

- prospects
- strategic client accounts
- account-performance dashboards
- high-priority client escalations
- executive business reviews

## Daily Lifecycle

### Step 1. Review business dashboard

- review prospect pipeline
- review active account health
- review meetings completed and outcomes
- review new requisition inflow from client activity

### Step 2. Review strategic account movement

- check stalled or high-value accounts
- inspect client-response delays
- inspect mandate conversion rates

### Step 3. Intervene where needed

- redirect priority toward strategic clients
- guide account teams on next steps
- support resolution of commercial blockers

## Weekly Lifecycle

- review account conversion
- review meeting-to-mandate ratios
- review client concentration and risk
- review location- or team-level business performance

## Monthly Lifecycle

- review strategic account growth
- review dormant account recovery
- review business pipeline quality
- review leadership-level performance trends

## Key Screens And Views

- business dashboard
- client and account summary views
- meetings and conversion reports
- strategic account health view

## Important Handoffs

- hands active account execution to `client_manager`
- communicates priority accounts to `delivery_lead`
- escalates setup or access needs to `platform_admin`

## Success Metrics

- meeting-to-mandate conversion
- active-account growth
- dormant-account recovery
- high-priority account retention
- strategic requisition conversion

## MVP Expectations

For MVP, this role should be able to:

- view business dashboard
- view owned or scoped clients
- review requisition inflow from accounts
- review strategic business performance
