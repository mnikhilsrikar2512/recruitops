# Recruiter Lifecycle

## Purpose

The `recruiter` role owns candidate delivery work: sourcing, screening, progressing candidates, and updating recruiting records.

## Typical Customer Title Mappings

- Recruiter
- Talent Partner
- Talent Acquisition Specialist
- Recruitment Executive

## Main Responsibilities

- work assigned requisitions
- source candidates
- maintain candidate records
- progress candidates through pipeline stages
- capture daily operational progress

## Primary Records Owned Or Controlled

- assigned requisitions
- candidate records
- candidate documents
- stage movements
- recruiter work queue
- candidate activity notes

## Daily Lifecycle

### Step 1. Review my work queue

- review assigned requisitions
- review candidates requiring action
- review overdue follow-ups
- review interviews and stage changes due today

### Step 2. Source and identify candidates

- search available channels
- review existing candidate pool
- contact or shortlist matching candidates

### Step 3. Create and update candidate records

- add candidate identity and source details
- upload resumes and documents
- update notes and ownership
- run duplicate checks where needed

### Step 4. Screen and progress candidates

- evaluate fit against requisition basics
- move candidates through stages
- mark hold, reject, or ready for next step
- prepare profiles for delivery review

### Step 5. Record blockers and progress

- note missing documents
- note poor candidate fit
- note client-feedback delay or interview delay
- update daily progress view

## Weekly Lifecycle

- review candidate movement
- review open assigned requisitions
- review submissions, interviews, and rejections
- review sourcing output

## Monthly Lifecycle

- review productivity trends
- review source effectiveness
- review closures and drop-offs
- review requisition load patterns

## Key Screens And Views

- my work dashboard
- requisition detail
- candidate list and candidate detail
- duplicate-check flow
- stage-transition workflow

## Important Handoffs

- receives requisition direction from `delivery_lead`
- may receive candidate drafts from `sourcing_support`
- hands reviewed candidates to `delivery_lead` or next delivery step
- receives client-side context indirectly through `client_manager` and `delivery_lead`

## Success Metrics

- candidate movement rate
- submission output
- interview progression rate
- quality of record completeness
- low duplicate creation rate

## MVP Expectations

For MVP, this role must be able to:

- view assigned requisitions
- create and update candidates
- upload documents
- move candidates through stages
- see my work dashboard
